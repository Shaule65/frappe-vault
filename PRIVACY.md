# Privacy Policy

Thank you for choosing to use Frappe Vault. We are committed to transparency and respect for your privacy. 

This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use Frappe Vault.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
Frappe Vault is a free and open-source application designed to be completely self-hosted. The developers and maintainers of Frappe Vault **do not collect, store, process, transmit, or have any access to any personal data, usage metrics, or analytics** from instances where this software is installed.

## 2. Self-Hosting and User Responsibility
Because Frappe Vault is self-hosted, all data processed by the application remains entirely on the server infrastructure that you control and operate. 

You, as the administrator of the self-hosted instance, are fully responsible for:
- The security and integrity of your server.
- The data you process and store using the application.
- Ensuring compliance with any applicable data protection and privacy laws (e.g., GDPR, CCPA) in your jurisdiction regarding the users of your instance.

## 3. Third-Party Integrations
If you configure Frappe Vault to interact with third-party services (e.g., cloud storage providers or external APIs), any data transmitted to those services is governed by their respective privacy policies. We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices of any third-party services you choose to integrate with your self-hosted instance.

## 4. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our project or legal requirements. Any updates will be published on our project repository.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to the community via our official GitHub repository: https://github.com/lubusIN/frappe-vault
